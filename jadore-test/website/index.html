The proposed contribution, explained

The proposed CHF 3 contribution would apply once to an eligible completed, paid direct stay. Cancelled, no-show, fully refunded and test stays would be excluded. The start date, payer, allocation and final eligibility terms must be confirmed before launch.

Choosing a cause would be optional after booking. The proposed default is literacy; skipping would not reduce the contribution. Every website language would offer the same amount and choices.

No transfers have been made under this proposal. Reports would show recipient-confirmed contributions, with dates. Books, meals, trees and lives would not be counted from bookings.

A few clear answers before you choose your stay.

Contact Our Team ↗
How do tourist taxes work?

Montreux has both a regional and a communal tourist tax. The amount depends on the accommodation, nights and taxable guests. Ask for an itemised breakdown and disclose any exemptions. If tax is already collected for your reservation, it should not be charged again.

Official Montreux Information ↗
What do I need for check-in?

Guest registration is part of the pre-arrival process. Your reservation link explains the information requested. Use that secure link for identity documents; contact the host if a link fails or you need help.

Is the Montreux Riviera Card included?

Ask the host to confirm eligibility, delivery and validity for your stay. Do this before arrival if you plan to use local transport. The official guide explains the card’s benefits and conditions.

Riviera Card Information ↗
Are parking and late check-in available?

These depend on the property. Check the specific listing and confirm your arrival time with the host. Parking is not included with every stay.

How can I cancel or change a booking?

Read the cancellation terms shown for your chosen stay before payment. They vary by reservation. For changes, use your confirmation details to contact the host.
