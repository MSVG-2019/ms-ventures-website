What is included in this apartment?

The listed capacity, bedrooms, bathroom and facilities above describe this accommodation. Check the current listing for bed arrangements, included services and any conditions that matter to your party. Additional services or charges must be checked for your specific reservation.

How do tourist taxes work?

Ask for an itemised breakdown for your accommodation, dates and taxable guests, including applicable exemptions. Check which amounts have already been collected and which remain due. A charge already collected for your reservation should not be charged again.

What happens before arrival?

Use the secure reservation link sent by the host to review guest registration and any outstanding tourist tax. Follow the instructions for this specific property, and contact the host through your reservation if a form or link fails. Identity documents belong in the approved secure registration flow.

Can I change or cancel my stay?

Read the terms shown with your chosen dates and price before payment. Payment schedules and cancellation conditions depend on the reservation. Use your confirmation details to contact the host about a change.
