Review Draft · Version 0.1.0 · 3 October 2026

A Riviera welcome. Clear from the first click.

This is the complete proposed J’adore Montreux guide for the website, guest journey and every related visual deliverable. It establishes a concrete system for review, not an approved corporate standard. Lake & Lavaux is the provisional recommendation. Belle Époque and Modern Riviera remain separate alternatives. All dimensions, proportions, weights and design specifications below are proposed. Brand approval, rights clearance and production-flow verification are separate decisions.

The existing brand-guidelines.md, jadore-wordmark.svg and jadore-favicon.svg are preserved. Their warm editorial identity is earlier proposed artwork; this document does not silently replace or approve it. The companion tokens.json records the new alternatives; SKILL.md makes these rules portable without installing them globally. The illustrated reader is site/brand-style-guide.html.

Latest creative preference: warmth leads all three directions. Cream, peach, rose and sunlit apricot dominate the interface. Lake blue, sage and powder are supporting accents. A Lake & Lavaux identity must feel like Riviera sunlight, not a predominantly cold-blue website; Modern must feel welcoming, not clinical. Preserve actual photographic colours rather than manufacturing a warm property image.

01 · Brand Idea and Positioning

J’adore invites guests into Montreux through real places, a personal welcome and practical care. The emotional promise is the pleasure of staying on the Swiss Riviera. The functional promise is clarity: understand the accommodation, booking total, registration, tourist taxes and arrival steps before they become a surprise.

Positioning: thoughtfully presented apartments and clearly labelled private rooms, with a coherent direct-booking and guest-preparation experience. Do not describe the whole collection as a hotel, palace, resort or five-star inventory. The aesthetic can be premium; the accommodation and services must remain specific to each listing.

PrincipleWhat the Guest Should ExperienceHow It Appears

Swiss clarityDetails that hold togetherConsistent names, dates, totals and predictable controls

Working invitation: Fall in love. Stay a little longer. It is expressive copy, not confirmation of an extension, minimum stay, discount or availability. Measure success through completed direct stays, contribution margin, guest comprehension and support friction; a palette or slogan does not prove an uplift.

04 · Logo and Asset Policy

The preserved master is the earlier outlined lowercase italic wordmark with Montreux descriptor and coral star. Its SVG canvas is 519.720 × 245 units. The small mark has a 64 × 64-unit canvas. Both are proposed, not approved. Their legacy deep lake #173D43 and coral #CB7462 remain embedded in the preserved artwork. Do not recolour them automatically to the new alternative palettes or present them as a newly approved Lake & Lavaux logo.

Proposed use specifications:

  • Full wordmark: minimum 120 CSS pixels wide digitally or 30 millimetres in print, with a readable descriptor at the actual output size. Keep its aspect ratio.
  • Clear space: at least the descriptor’s height around the full mark; one quarter of the tile width around the small mark.
  • Small visible mark: at least 24 CSS pixels. Inspect the 16-pixel favicon exception on light/dark browser chrome.
  • Use a quiet light presentation surface. A reverse master has not been supplied: commission/review one before dark placement, rather than applying a blanket filter.
  • No stretching, rotation, outlined tracing, drop shadow, extra slogan inside the artwork or attachment of a group logo/Swiss flag. The star is decoration, never a rating, certification, payment status or charitable endorsement.
  • Informative alt text: “J’adore Montreux”. A linked home logo also needs a meaningful home destination/name. Decorative repeats use empty alt text.

Asset register: brand masters remain in brand/jadore-wordmark.svg and brand/jadore-favicon.svg; site copies are in site/assets/. Keep a source/master, versioned approved variants, export size/format and rights record. SVG is preferred for vector artwork; PNG serves raster-only clients; JPG/WebP is for photographs. Rasterising an asset is an export, not a new design approval. Never substitute screenshot logos or an asset marked superseded.

05 · Colour, Contrast and Semantics

Use warm paper for continuity, real photography for atmosphere and selected warm pastel panels for rhythm. A proposed starting balance is approximately 55% cream/light surfaces, 25% real photography, 15% pastel panels dominated by sun/peach, rose/porcelain or butter/coral, and 5% dark/decorative accents. Cool lake/vine/verdigris/powder/pistachio stay subordinate rather than covering consecutive major sections. Judge the composition, not pixels. Keep the booking action stronger than the decorative palette.

Full pair calculations and unrounded test decisions are recorded in tokens.json.

Aim for at least 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for qualifying large text and clear non-text control information. Do not round a failed value into a pass. Actual rendered transparency, overlays, images, fonts and interaction states need their own check. A table of solid pairs is not an accessibility certification. Keep essential text dark; do not put fees or warnings in faint pastel type. Underline contextual links and identify status in words as well as colour. W3C contrast guidance

Shared proposed status roles: error #A33735, confirmed success #31594F, caution #745521, each on the selected paper with a matching word/icon. “Success” means an actually verified state; a layout concept says “Example status”. Decorative dividers may use a quiet ink tint; a required field boundary must use the tested action/ink colour. Focus uses an offset two-tone ring: a light separation plus a strong contrasting outer edge. On dark panels, invert the ring and test it against both adjacent surfaces.

07 · Voice and Copy

Warm, specific, calmly useful. Emotion belongs in the invitation; precision belongs in the decision. Speak directly, use short connected sentences and give each instruction its purpose. In English, use sentence case for actions and headings; preserve proper names. Avoid bureaucratic acronyms in guest-facing instructions. French and German use a respectful form consistently; Spanish tone and Chinese wording need editorial/native review before adoption.

Action vocabulary: Find your stay · View stay details · Review your stay · Guest registration · Tourist taxes · Arrival details · Contact your host. A contextual external handoff should explain its purpose. Do not promise a response time, staff hours or a feature merely because a provider account is connected. A useful failure message states what happened, preserves safe inputs and gives a verified next step.

Example invitation: “Morning coffee at your own pace. A walk along the lake. Choose the space that suits your Montreux stay.” Example uncertainty: “Lift access has not been confirmed for this listing. Check with the host if it matters for your stay.” Example help: “If your secure link does not work, contact the host through your reservation.”

08 · Prices, Taxes, Offers and Charitable Claims

Show a total only from the approved live quote for the chosen property, dates and party. State currency, stay/night basis, included/excluded amounts and actual cancellation/refund conditions. Separate accommodation, cleaning, applicable taxes, deposit/preauthorisation and optional extras. Editorial money uses readable CHF 3; a quote, invoice or receipt retains validated monetary precision and locale formatting. Do not round a tax, percentage or payment to fit a graphic rule. A deposit is not accommodation revenue or a charitable base.

Tourist taxes require separate regional/communal explanation, guest eligibility/exemptions, dates, classification and channel reconciliation. Historical rates or a worked example are not a current reservation demand. Never use an old blanket total or collect again without considering what the original channel already collected. Distinguish taxes from optional minibar purchases, extensions and late checkout. Identity data, signed payment links and entry/Wi-Fi details belong in the approved private reservation flow.

The proposed 5% direct offer is inactive. Any launch needs a verified accommodation-only base, eligible booking channel/period/stays, exclusions, minimum/maximum rules if used, cancellation/refund treatment, taxes/fees, stacking and margin approval. A best-price guarantee requires an actual comparison and enforceable terms; do not invent one. Never preload paid extras, conceal compulsory charges or manufacture scarcity.

The proposed CHF 3 giving programme is inactive. Current candidates are Room to Read for literacy and GiveWell Top Charities Fund for evidence-backed health. No partnership or arrangement is agreed. Copy must say company-funded, no extra guest fee and eligible completed/paid direct stay; the amount, payer, recipient permissions and final conditions remain pending. Optional cause choice would follow booking; skipping retains the proposed company literacy default without reducing the contribution. Use the same amount/choices in all five languages. A public preview saves, sends and donates nothing.

Before activation, define start/enrollment, net amount reaching the recipient, commercially covered sender fees/FX, completed-stay eligibility and refunds. A full refund before transfer differs from a refund after a recipient-confirmed gift; do not erase the historical donation or imply clawback. Public proof comes from confirmed recipient receipts, not booking counts, pending card debits or historical Airbnb stays. Do not convert CHF into books, meals, trees or lives, issue the guest a donation tax receipt, imply Foundation funding, or promise statistically guaranteed booking uplift. An approved charity arrangement cannot substitute for tested payment/ledger controls.

AI help is also a distinct readiness question: the existing prototype remains disabled for live AI. A public concierge can cite approved public facts and explain preparation, but does not identify a guest, authorise access, settle taxes or change a reservation. Label a demonstration and route private matters to the host. Do not use “24/7 concierge” to imply staffed or verified service without evidence.

09 · Photography, Illustration and Rights

Actual accommodation comes first. Bind each photograph to its exact listing, space, date, source and allowed use. The reviewed collection contains listing records that can overlap; fifteen cards do not prove fifteen unique, simultaneously available units. Seven private-room records must not borrow whole-apartment labels or unverified bathroom exclusivity. A studio with an unknown bedroom count must not become a one-bedroom apartment through copy or staging.

Shoot natural light, honest materials and useful angles. Include sleeping arrangements, kitchen/bathroom context, shared spaces and access information when relevant. Preserve roofwork, lift/no-lift, room-assignment and dated/weather-related cautions; a beautiful crop cannot conceal an operational limitation. Avoid excessive warmth, wide-angle distortion and removal of permanent features. Do not invent furnishings, enlarge rooms, add a view or use generated photographs as documentary property evidence.

Destination imagery belongs in an identified destination section. The preserved Chillon sunset asset is a destination image, not a balcony/window view. Lake, Lavaux and Belle Époque references guide original art direction, but tourism/hotel site availability is not a licence to download, publish or modify their photos/marks. Use original commissioned images or expressly licensed assets with required credits. No tourism logo, UNESCO emblem, hotel crest or named-charity logo is part of the J’adore system.

Record creator, licence/permission, permitted channels, expiry, attribution, property/model consent where applicable and any edits. Confirm commercial rights separately from the existence of a locally downloaded file. Keep research screenshots in research, outside public release. Obtain specific testimonial/portrait permission; do not republish guest messages, names, faces or reservation details from operational records simply because feedback was received. Accessibility descriptions identify the useful scene, not an invented marketing benefit.

Proposed exports: landscape property cards near 4:3 or 16:10, with an original uncropped master; destination heroes near 16:9 plus a separately checked mobile crop. Verify the actual rendered crop at 390 pixels. Set intrinsic image dimensions, compress without obscuring evidence and provide suitable responsive sizes. An export dimension is a working design specification, not a claim about the original file or approved usage.

10 · Layout and Component States

Proposed layout: 1,280-pixel maximum content width; 64-pixel desktop outer space, 24-pixel mobile outer space; 12/8/4-column working grids. Use a spacing rhythm of 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 96 pixels. Break at content pressure rather than a named device. A starting responsive range is compact below 640 pixels, intermediate to 1,023 pixels and wide from 1,024 pixels; verify wrapping, zoom and safe areas. Use 12–20-pixel card corners for Lake/Modern, and more restrained 4–10-pixel corners for Belle Époque. These values guide a future implementation; they are not measurements of the current prototype.

One primary booking action should be visible before the hero photograph on mobile. Show property type/capacity and a route to all stays early. Never let a charity invitation, offer concept, floating widget or consent panel obscure it. Filters state capacity fit, not live date availability. Preserve valid entered dates/party across native booking links, detail pages, catalogue and language changes; invalid, partial, past or reversed periods require local correction without a misleading handoff. Never silently clamp an over-capacity party.

Controls use real semantic elements, not a clickable-looking picture. A demonstration in this style book is visibly static. Test initial, success, empty, validation, late-load, cancellation and reopen paths. In particular, a delayed close must not clear a newly reopened dialog; a preview button must not appear ready before its deferred initializer is bound. A persistent public QR or link must never reveal entry instructions or guest identity.

11 · Accessible Experience and Motion

Target a usable WCAG 2.2 AA experience, supported by actual review and representative runtime checks; do not put a conformance badge on a guide alone. Maintain reading order, one meaningful page title, logical heading levels, a skip link and clear landmarks. Keyboard users must reach every action and understand focus. Visible labels and text errors complement accessible names and live announcements. Avoid redundant announcements and decorative content in the reading order.

The proposed 44-pixel action height is J’adore’s usability preference; WCAG 2.2’s minimum target criterion is 24 × 24 CSS pixels subject to its exceptions, not a universal 44-pixel AA rule. Check cramped spacing as well as size. Test 320-pixel reflow, 200% text enlargement and 400% browser zoom where applicable; do not rely on a single 390-pixel screenshot. Essential labels/terms must wrap without horizontal scrolling; factual comparison tables need an accessible solution when necessary. W3C target guidance, reflow, focus not obscured

Use motion only to clarify a transition: proposed 120–180 milliseconds for small state changes and 200–300 milliseconds for a gentle panel entrance. Respect reduced-motion settings by removing nonessential movement. No autoplay audio, forced video, parallax scrolling, flashing status or looping urgency. A photo can be still. Do not animate totals, eligibility, payment confirmation or public charitable counts into a false impression of progress. Text, reading order and controls remain usable if animation or custom fonts fail.

12 · Every Format, One Welcome

Website and Guest Portal

Use the selected tokens consistently across homepage, catalogue, details and help. A public website contains public listing/general guidance only. Reservation-specific identity, payment, access and support belong in approved private systems. Provider pages may be branded only within actual configurable scope; inspect global/per-property overrides, legal merchant labels and both desktop/mobile outputs. A styled provider preview does not prove a completed guest flow. Preserve the original booking channel: Airbnb help must not introduce an off-platform Hostaway payment link.

Email and Guest Messages

For an approved HTML email, use a proposed 600-pixel single-column content width, light paper surface, compact logo, 24–32-pixel headline, 16-pixel body and one primary task. Provide meaningful plain-text content when images/fonts are blocked. Verify in actual mail clients and provider templates before sending. Plain-text/OTA messages carry the voice and signature without pasted web CSS. Do not overload a guest with duplicated forms or provider links.

Draft sample · no message sent:

“Your Montreux stay is a little closer. Please use the secure link in your reservation to check guest registration, review tourist taxes already collected and find your property’s arrival details. If a step does not work, contact us through your original reservation. À bientôt, The J’adore Montreux Team.”

Required legal sender details must name the verified contracting entity separately from the guest-facing team. Dates, party, property and amounts need real reservation data in a private, approved template. Never insert a real passport, door code, guest name or payment token in a public example.

Arrival Guide and In-Room QR

Order the guide: correct property/stay, help contact, preparation status, registration, itemised taxes/previous collections, arrival time/directions and private access details. Say who needs to act and why. A checklist template is not live completion status. Show one secure next step, preserve previous information and offer a verified fallback.

An in-room card carries one task, a short readable URL and an actual tested QR destination. Proposed print working size: A6, with 22-millimetre QR square plus adequate quiet zone; confirm scanning on the physical print and actual phones. A payment QR must identify the real merchant and whether the purchase is optional or a reconciled mandatory amount. Publicly posted cards must not encode guest-specific tokens or persistent building codes. Avoid unapproved shorteners and make the purpose clear before scanning.

Social and Campaigns

Proposed working canvases: 1,080 × 1,350 pixels for a portrait feed composition and 1,080 × 1,920 for a story; verify each platform’s current placement/crop requirements before publishing. Use one true property or labelled destination photo, one short message and a clear approved booking destination. Keep important text/logo away from the interface edges. Do not bake compulsory terms into unreadable image text; provide accessible copy/alt text and linked conditions. Guest likenesses, testimonials, music and partner marks need rights clearance.

Word, PDF and Printed Collateral

Proposed A4 portrait document: 20-millimetre margins, a compact brand header, display-family headings, 11–12-point body, 1.45–1.6 line spacing and page-number/version footer. Use real headings and selectable text; set document language, preserve links and inspect accessible/tagged PDF output. Make legal information as readable as the welcome. Check greyscale, actual print size, logo edges and photo licence/credit. Print colour conversion/proofing is required; the RGB hex palette is not a Pantone or CMYK specification.

PowerPoint

Proposed 16:9 canvas: 13.333 × 7.5 inches. One main point per slide; 32–44-point heading, 22–28-point body, and at least 18-point meaningful supporting text where practical. Use selected theme colours and the display/Inter pair, with documented fallbacks on the recipient’s machine. Keep photos honest and labels readable in a room. No partner-family org chart or property-count claim from old source material. Check every slide after font substitution and in PDF export; a theme setting does not verify overflow.

Excel, Tables, Charts and Reports

Use Inter or the tested Office fallback at a proposed 11–12 points, a dark-ink header with light text, light zebra rows and restrained separators. Freeze useful headers, label currency/period and align comparable numbers. Keep totals explicit, source dates visible and unknown values distinct from zero. Do not round money or percentages to satisfy an inherited finance-brand rule. Charts use direct labels, readable axes and words/shapes as well as colour; a pastel panel is not a data series. Separate observed direct bookings, OTA bookings, modelled scenarios and contribution margin; do not seed an impact counter from forecasts.

13 · Ownership, Versions and Approval

Proposed stewardship: the brand owner designates the final brand approver; the hospitality operator maintains listing/guest truth; the contracting host/merchant validates commercial disclosures; technical owners validate delivery/access/payment; qualified reviewers check language, rights and accessibility. No approver appointment is asserted by this draft.

Version the book, tokens, logo variants, translations and templates together. Use patch changes for a correction that does not change identity/terms; minor changes for added formats/components; major changes for the adopted identity or material commercial promises. Keep a dated decision/change register with the author, approver, affected assets, evidence and migration status. Preserve earlier versions for comparison; do not overwrite masters while concepts are undecided.

14 · Pre-Delivery and Launch Review

Before a deliverable is handed over:

  • Correct guest/corporate identity and exact names; no invented merchant or group relationship.
  • Visible version/status; selected concept and tokens match throughout.
  • Original/current asset masters, correct aspect ratio and a recorded rights basis.
  • One Latin display/UI pair plus deliberate script coverage; readable five-language text, dates and amounts.
  • Rendered contrast, keyboard focus, zoom/reflow, accessible names and meaningful state messages checked.
  • Real property/capacity/category/photos/cautions; no borrowed rating or fabricated availability.
  • Actual price/tax/discount/charity meaning preserved; proposed features clearly inactive.
  • Responsive and print/export outputs inspected at their actual size; no hidden truncation.
  • Source files and a readable preview saved; no credentials, guest records or private access instructions in a public artifact.
  • Independent review, material findings, meaningful test evidence and remaining limits recorded for critical features.

Before production: approve the chosen concept and final artwork; confirm rights, contracting host/merchant and support; align all five language/template/provider touchpoints; test actual booking quotes/handoffs, original-channel guidance, registration/tax/payment exceptions, refunds and notifications; verify access boundaries and current terms. Record the exact release and real target. An internal style-book review does not authorise global installation, wider-team messaging, cloud publication, donations or production access changes.
