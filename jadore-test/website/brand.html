01 / Colour

Lake light.
Warm welcome.

Warm ivory, peach and blush bring a softer Riviera light. Deep lake anchors the words and actions. Terracotta adds a human note; airy lake and sage surfaces leave room for the place itself.

Colour serves clarity. Deep lake on ivory has 11.25:1 contrast; terracotta on ivory has 5.97:1. Coral is a decorative accent and does not carry small text or essential instructions.

02 / Type & Voice

A little poetry.
Perfectly clear.

Newsreader gives the story a graceful rhythm. Inter handles the details with care. Expressive headlines open the door; concise, useful language helps a guest choose their stay and arrive with confidence.

NEWSREADER · THE INVITATION

Fall in love.
Stay a little longer.

INTER · THE DETAILS

Your chosen stay. A clear price. The information you need before you travel. Thoughtful hospitality starts with making the next step easy.

Our StaysYour ArrivalContact Our Team ↗

Warm and specific. One idea per sentence. Never promise a view, parking, a discount or a guest benefit that the reservation cannot verify.

03 / Photography

The real place.
The right feeling.

Natural light, honest spaces and details worth noticing. Property images show the actual stay. Destination images tell the Riviera story, with a clear caption.

Let the photograph do the inviting.

Preserve the true room, furnishings and view. Use warmth gently. Show shared spaces and private rooms as clearly as entire apartments.

04 / In Use

One welcome.
Every touchpoint.

The brand follows the guest beyond the homepage. A short message, a secure next step and an arrival guide that puts the useful information first. These are layout concepts, not live messages or a connected guest portal.
