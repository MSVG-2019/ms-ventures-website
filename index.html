The group

An Investment House, Held for Generations

M&S Ventures Group is a privately owned investment and holding group, established in 2019 and based in the Lake Geneva region. It is wholly owned by its founder, Milind Madan, and invests only its own capital. It retains full, independent control of its investment decisions.

The group holds three kinds of asset: Swiss property, controlling interests in Swiss companies, and investments in public securities worldwide. Its manner across all three is the same — a patient, principal owner that carefully commits capital, then steps back, leaving each building, business and holding to the leaders who know it best.

How we own

The Long View, Privately Held.

We measure ownership in years, not quarters. Whether the asset is a building, a business or a share, our part is the same: to commit capital carefully, then entrust it to the leaders who run it best. We take no operational or management decisions.

The people

Founded, Owned and Led.

M&S Ventures Group is founded and wholly owned by Milind Madan, and led day to day by its Managing Director and co-founder, Kim Scheffer. As owners the group stays patient and non-interfering in the companies it holds; behind that stance sit two people who set its direction and steward its capital.
